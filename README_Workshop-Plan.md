## 1. 👋 Introductions — 15 min

- Who are the facilitators?
- Participant intros: name + favourite math concept
- History of generative art ([Andy Warhol and the Amiga](https://www.warhol.org/exhibition/warhol-and-the-amiga/))
- Intro to Processing

## 2. 💻 Intro to p5.js — 15 min

- `x`, `y`, canvas
- `setup()`, `draw()`
- `width`, `height`, `mouseX`, `mouseY`

## 3. 🔲 10 PRINT demo & explanation — 15 min

- Walk through the 10 PRINT sketches

## 4. 🧪 Experiment with 10 PRINT — 15 min

- Hands-on: participants remix the 10 PRINT sketch

## 5. 〰️ Truchet tiles — 15 min

## 6. 🎨 Image manipulation through pointillism — 30 min

> 🖼️ It took Georges Seurat roughly two years to complete *A Sunday Afternoon on the Island of La Grande Jatte*.

- Reveal an image with `blend` and `mask`
- Use pixel RGB info
- Webcam

## 7. 🌀 Spiral — 15 min

- Use brightness data to map onto the spiral and reveal the image

## 8. ♾️ Recursion & Hilbert curve — 30 min

- Recursion with recursive circles
- Hilbert curve with recursion
- Show the non-recursive version as another possibility
- Show the Mandelbrot set, Sierpiński triangle, Perlin noise and Fourier series as possibilities
